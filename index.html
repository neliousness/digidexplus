DigiDexPlus

Every Digimon, every evolution.

Unofficial fan-made encyclopedia

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

  • Draw your partner: spin the card dial to meet a random Digimon.
  • Over 1,400 Digimon with artwork, level, type, attribute, debut year and profile.
  • Evolutions: what each Digimon evolves from and into.
  • Fields and skills on one page.
  • Search and filter: by name, by number (#001) or by level.
  • Favorites, kept on your device.

Credits

Digimon names, profiles and images come from the community-run Digi-API. The app is free and supported by ads.
